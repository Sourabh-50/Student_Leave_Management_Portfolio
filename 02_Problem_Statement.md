# Problem Statement

## 1. Current Process Overview
Currently, the institution employs a highly manual, paper-driven process for student leave management. 

1. **Initiation:** The student obtains a physical leave application form from the administration office.
2. **Form Filling:** The student fills out personal details, leave dates, and the reason for the leave, attaching any physical proof (like a medical certificate) if required.
3. **Faculty Approval:** The student must physically locate their Class Coordinator or Faculty Advisor to get the first signature.
4. **HOD Approval:** Depending on the duration of the leave (e.g., >3 days), the form must then be physically taken to the Head of Department (HOD) for a secondary signature.
5. **Final Submission:** The fully signed form is submitted to the Administration office, where a clerk manually updates the student's attendance ledger.

## 2. Pain Points
This manual workflow presents several severe pain points across all stakeholder groups:

- **Students:** Wasted time physically tracking down faculty members; anxiety over lost forms; no way to check if leave is approved without visiting the admin office.
- **Faculty/HODs:** Interruption of academic work to review and sign physical forms; inability to quickly verify a student's past leave history before approving a new request.
- **Administration:** High volume of manual data entry leading to errors; physical storage constraints for paper forms; difficulty in compiling monthly attendance reports.
- **Management:** Complete lack of real-time visibility into overall student absenteeism rates.

## 3. Business Impact
The inefficiencies in the current process have a direct negative impact on the institution's operations and reputation:

| Impact Area | Description | Severity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Cost** | High administrative overhead due to manual data entry, paper procurement, and physical document storage. | High |
| **Data Accuracy** | Manual ledgers are prone to human error, resulting in incorrect attendance calculations which can affect student grading and exam eligibility. | Critical |
| **Productivity Loss** | Faculty and students waste valuable academic hours managing administrative bureaucracy rather than focusing on education. | Medium |
| **Compliance Risk** | Lost documents mean the institution cannot prove compliance with mandatory attendance regulations set by educational boards. | High |

## 4. Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)
To understand the core issue, a "5 Whys" analysis was conducted on the problem of delayed and lost leave applications:

1. **Why are leave applications frequently delayed or lost?**
   - Because they are processed using physical paper forms that pass through multiple hands.
2. **Why are physical paper forms used?**
   - Because there is no digital system in place to capture and route the requests.
3. **Why is there no digital system in place?**
   - Because the legacy process was never updated as the student population grew.
4. **Why wasn't the legacy process updated?**
   - Because there was a lack of clear ownership over the end-to-end attendance management process.
5. **Why was there a lack of ownership? (Root Cause)**
   - **Root Cause:** The institution has historically viewed leave management as a fragmented, departmental administrative task rather than a centralized, institution-wide digital workflow requiring a dedicated technology solution.
