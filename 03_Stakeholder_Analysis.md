# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Stakeholder Profiles

### 1.1 Students
*   **Responsibilities:** Submit accurate leave requests, provide necessary documentation (e.g., medical certificates), and monitor their own attendance thresholds.
*   **Pain Points:** Time wasted finding faculty for signatures; lack of transparency on request status; lost paper forms.
*   **Goals:** Quick, hassle-free leave application process; real-time status updates; mobile-friendly access.

### 1.2 Faculty (Class Coordinators)
*   **Responsibilities:** Review and approve/reject leave requests for their assigned students (usually for short-term leaves 1-3 days); monitor class attendance.
*   **Pain Points:** Constant interruptions to sign forms; no easy way to check a student's prior leave history during the approval process.
*   **Goals:** Fast, one-click approval process; easy access to student attendance history; automated routing to HOD if required.

### 1.3 Head of Department (HOD)
*   **Responsibilities:** Approve long-term leaves (e.g., >3 days) or special leaves; monitor overall attendance trends within their department.
*   **Pain Points:** Bottleneck of paperwork on their desk; lack of department-wide reporting on absenteeism.
*   **Goals:** Summary dashboards of pending requests; ability to delegate approvals when out of office; analytical reports.

### 1.4 Administration (Admin Staff)
*   **Responsibilities:** Finalize leave records; update the central attendance system; maintain compliance records.
*   **Pain Points:** Manual data entry from paper forms; handling student complaints about lost forms; archiving physical paper.
*   **Goals:** Automated data syncing with the attendance database; digital, searchable archives; elimination of manual entry.

### 1.5 IT Team
*   **Responsibilities:** System deployment, maintenance, user management, and technical support.
*   **Pain Points:** Supporting fragmented legacy systems; ensuring data security and privacy.
*   **Goals:** A scalable, secure, and easy-to-maintain cloud-based solution; clear documentation.

### 1.6 Principal / Management
*   **Responsibilities:** Oversee institution-wide operations; ensure regulatory compliance regarding student attendance.
*   **Pain Points:** Lack of high-level visibility into institutional metrics; inability to track trends over time.
*   **Goals:** High-level executive dashboards; improved operational efficiency; cost reduction.

## 2. Power / Interest Matrix

| Stakeholder | Power | Interest | Strategy (Engagement Approach) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Principal / Management** | High | High | **Manage Closely:** Key sponsors. Require regular updates on ROI and project milestones. |
| **HOD** | High | High | **Manage Closely:** Critical for workflow approval. Need their buy-in for process changes. |
| **Administration** | Medium | High | **Keep Informed:** End-users of the final data. Need comprehensive training. |
| **Faculty** | Medium | High | **Keep Informed:** High interaction volume. Need a seamless UX to ensure adoption. |
| **Students** | Low | High | **Keep Informed:** Primary users. Need clear communication and onboarding. |
| **IT Team** | High | Medium | **Keep Satisfied:** Crucial for implementation and security approvals. |
