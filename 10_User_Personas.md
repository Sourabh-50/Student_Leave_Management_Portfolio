# User Personas

---

## Persona 1: The Student (Primary User)
**Name:** Rahul Sharma  
**Role:** 2nd Year Engineering Student  
**Age:** 20  
**Tech Savvy:** High (Mobile-first user)

*   **Goals:** 
    *   Wants to apply for leave quickly without running around campus.
    *   Wants to know instantly if his leave is approved so he can plan his travel.
    *   Wants to maintain his required 75% attendance threshold.
*   **Challenges:** 
    *   Has to skip classes just to find the HOD for a signature.
    *   Often forgets to carry the physical medical certificate.
*   **Frustrations:** 
    *   "I submitted my form three days ago and have no idea if the Faculty signed it yet."
    *   "Admin lost my paper, and now I'm marked absent for the week."
*   **Needs & Behaviors:** 
    *   Needs a mobile-friendly interface.
    *   Expects push or email notifications.

## Persona 2: The Faculty (First-level Approver)
**Name:** Prof. Anita Desai  
**Role:** Class Coordinator / Assistant Professor  
**Age:** 38  
**Tech Savvy:** Medium (Prefers Desktop/Laptop)

*   **Goals:** 
    *   Wants to manage student leaves efficiently without disrupting lecture prep time.
    *   Needs to ensure students aren't abusing the leave policy.
*   **Challenges:** 
    *   Constant physical interruptions in the staff room by students holding forms.
    *   Cannot easily remember a student's past leave history when asked to sign.
*   **Frustrations:** 
    *   "I spend 30 minutes a day just signing paper forms."
    *   "I approved a leave but Admin didn't record it, so the student is blaming me."
*   **Needs & Behaviors:** 
    *   Needs a clean, bulk-approval dashboard.
    *   Requires a quick summary of the student's current attendance percentage before approving.

## Persona 3: The Head of Department (Second-level Approver)
**Name:** Dr. Vikram Singh  
**Role:** HOD - Computer Science  
**Age:** 52  
**Tech Savvy:** Low to Medium

*   **Goals:** 
    *   Wants to monitor overall departmental attendance and academic discipline.
    *   Only wants to deal with exceptions (long leaves, medical emergencies).
*   **Challenges:** 
    *   Desk is cluttered with paperwork.
    *   Lacks data to identify chronic absenteeism trends in the department.
*   **Frustrations:** 
    *   "Why am I signing a 1-day leave form? Faculty should handle this."
    *   "I have to ask Admin to manually generate a report if I want to see attendance trends."
*   **Needs & Behaviors:** 
    *   Needs automated routing so he only sees leaves >3 days.
    *   Values high-level analytical dashboards and charts.

## Persona 4: The Admin (Data Processor)
**Name:** Priya Patel  
**Role:** Academic Office Clerk  
**Age:** 29  
**Tech Savvy:** Medium (Proficient in Excel and Data Entry)

*   **Goals:** 
    *   Maintain accurate and compliant student records.
    *   Automate repetitive data entry tasks.
*   **Challenges:** 
    *   Deciphering bad handwriting on paper forms.
    *   Physically filing and retrieving hundreds of paper documents a month.
*   **Frustrations:** 
    *   "Typing data from paper into the system takes hours and causes eye strain."
    *   "During audits, finding a specific student's medical certificate from 6 months ago is a nightmare."
*   **Needs & Behaviors:** 
    *   Needs the system to automatically sync approved leaves to the database.
    *   Requires a robust search and filter feature to find historical records instantly.
