# Business Process Improvement (BPI) Summary

This document quantifies the value delivered by transitioning from the manual AS-IS process to the automated TO-BE process (SLMS).

## 1. Before vs. After Analysis

| Metric / Aspect | Before (AS-IS) | After (TO-BE) | Improvement Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Approval Turnaround Time (TAT)** | 72 - 120 Hours | < 24 Hours | ~80% Faster |
| **Data Entry Method** | Manual Ledger Entry | Automated DB Sync | 100% Automated |
| **Document Storage** | Physical Filing Cabinets | Cloud (AWS S3) | Zero physical space |
| **Status Transparency** | Zero (Verbal check-ins) | Real-time Dashboard | High Transparency |
| **Loss of Documents** | High frequency | Zero (Digital files) | 100% Secure |

## 2. Expected Benefits & ROI

### 2.1 Time Savings (Efficiency Improvement)
*   **Faculty:** Previously spent ~30 mins/day reviewing and signing physical forms. With bulk digital approval, this is reduced to ~5 mins/day. **(83% time saved per Faculty member).**
*   **Admin Staff:** Previously spent ~15 hours/week doing manual data entry of leave forms into the system. **(100% time saved - 15 hours repurposed for higher-value tasks).**

### 2.2 Cost Savings
*   **Paper & Printing:** Elimination of ~5,000 physical leave forms per semester.
*   **Storage:** Elimination of the need to purchase physical filing cabinets and dedicate floor space for document archiving.
*   **Administrative Overhead:** Reduced need for temporary data-entry clerks during peak times.

### 2.3 Qualitative Improvements
*   **Improved Compliance:** The immutable digital audit trail ensures that the institution can easily pass academic audits regarding student attendance requirements.
*   **Student Satisfaction:** Students experience less friction, reduced anxiety over lost documents, and do not have to skip classes to hunt down approvers.
*   **Strategic Insights:** Management now has a real-time pulse on campus health and absenteeism trends, enabling proactive interventions.
