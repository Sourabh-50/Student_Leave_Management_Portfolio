**Slide 2: The Problem (AS-IS)**
- Manual, paper-based leave process.
- 3-5 days average approval time.
- Lost documents and lack of transparency.
- Heavy manual data entry burden on Admin.

**Slide 3: Project Objectives**
- Digitize 100% of leave workflows.
- Reduce TAT to under 24 hours.
- Eliminate manual data entry.
- Provide real-time analytics for Management.

**Slide 4: Key Stakeholders**
- **Students:** Primary users.
- **Faculty:** Tier 1 Approvers.
- **HOD:** Tier 2 Approvers (Exceptions/Long leaves).
- **Admin:** Process owners and data managers.

**Slide 5: Proposed Solution (TO-BE)**
- A secure, role-based web portal.
- Automated routing based on business rules.
- Real-time email notifications.
- Integrated dashboards.

**Slide 6: Business Rules Implemented**
- Rule 1: <= 3 Days = Faculty Approval only.
- Rule 2: > 3 Days = Faculty + HOD Approval.
- Rule 3: Medical leaves require mandatory PDF upload.
